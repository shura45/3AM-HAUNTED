Heads up: this page has a jump scare with a loud sound. Turn your volume down if you are easily startled.

What is this?

This is a one-page website that works like a Halloween party invite. It has a countdown to Halloween night, a trick or treat game and a few hidden surprises. The goal was to make a page that feels creepy while still being easy to read and use.

Features
Flickering title: the main heading flickers like a dying lightbulb and glitches every few seconds.
Flashlight effect: the page is dark, and you can only see clearly around your mouse (or your finger on a phone).
Glowing eyes: red eyes blink in the dark corners of the screen.
Floating ghosts and drifting fog: small animations that make the background feel alive.
Live countdown: shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds until October 31 at 7:00 PM. The numbers glitch every second, and the message under them changes depending on how close the party is.
Trick or treat game: click "Knock on the door" to get a random result. Some are sweet and some are not. The same result never shows up twice in a row.
Pumpkin jump scare: the page tells you not to click the pumpkin. Click it anyway and you will see why.
Party details: a section with the when, where, what to wear and what to bring.
